About

Possibly still alive

I am an undergraduate student in Electronic Science and Technology at South China Agricultural University, based in Guangzhou. My recent work focuses on embodied AI, especially vision-language-action (VLA) systems and world models. I have completely mastered the art of balancing research and life. Let me strike that out until it is true. For now, closing my laptop for five minutes counts as progress.

Over the past year, I worked on agricultural foundation models in Professor Yubin Lan's lab at South China Agricultural University, collecting field images in lychee orchards, building multimodal question-answering data, helping design Lychee-Bench / Lychee-Mind, and training Qwen2.5-VL-7B with SFT and GRPO. I then interned at City University of Hong Kong, contributing to SemBid on language representations for auto-bidding, PhysicsMind for unified real-and-simulated physical reasoning evaluation, and Latent Reward Steering for inference-time reasoning guidance. Later, I joined the HCP Lab at Sun Yat-sen University to study tactile perception and world models. I will next join Fudan University to continue my studies and research under the supervision of Professor Ruoxi Jiang.
